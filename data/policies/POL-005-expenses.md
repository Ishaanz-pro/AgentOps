# Expense Reimbursement Policy (POL-005)

## 1. Eligible expenses
Work-related travel, meals during travel (per-diem ₹1,500/day domestic), client entertainment with prior approval, and home-office equipment up to **₹25,000 per financial year**.

## 2. Submission
Claims must be submitted within **60 days** of the expense date with itemised receipts. Claims without receipts above ₹500 are rejected.

## 3. Approval
Manager approval is required for all claims. Claims above ₹50,000 also require Finance Director approval.

## 4. Payment
Approved claims are paid with the next payroll cycle, typically within 15 business days.

## 5. Non-reimbursable
Alcohol (unless client entertainment), fines, personal purchases, and upgrades to premium travel classes without approval.
