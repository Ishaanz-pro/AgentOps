# Employee Data Privacy and Access Policy (POL-003)

## 1. Principle of least privilege
Employees may access only the records they own. Support agents may access records within their assigned region. Administrators may access all records for operational purposes and are audited quarterly.

## 2. Order records
Order records contain the owner's name, delivery address, items, and payment amount. They are classified **Internal – Restricted**. Sharing another employee's order details, even verbally, is a policy violation.

## 3. Assistant and automation access
Automated assistants must enforce the same access rules as the Employee Portal. An assistant must never disclose an order that the requesting user is not authorised to view, regardless of how the request is phrased.

## 4. Logging
Access to restricted records is logged with the requester's user ID, timestamp, and the record ID. Logs must not contain payment card data, full addresses, or authentication tokens.

## 5. Reporting
Suspected unauthorised access should be reported to security@example.internal (synthetic address) within 24 hours.
