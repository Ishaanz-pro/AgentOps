# Refund and Return Policy (POL-001)

## 1. Eligibility window
A refund may be requested within **30 calendar days of the delivery date** shown on the order record. Orders outside this window are not eligible for a refund but may qualify for a warranty claim under POL-004.

## 2. Order status requirements
- Orders in status `delivered` are eligible if within the 30-day window.
- Orders in status `shipped` or `processing` cannot be refunded; the employee should cancel the order instead (see POL-002 Section 4). Cancellation of a `shipped` order is treated as a return once the parcel is received back.
- Orders in status `cancelled` are automatically refunded within 5–7 business days; no further request is needed.

## 3. Condition of goods
Items must be returned unused, in original packaging, with all accessories. Opened software, consumables, and personalised items are non-refundable.

## 4. Refund method and timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after the returned item passes inspection at the regional warehouse.

## 5. Partial refunds
A partial refund (up to 20% deduction) may be applied when an item is returned with missing accessories or damaged packaging. The deduction is decided by the regional warehouse supervisor.

## 6. How to request
Employees request a refund through the Employee Portal → Orders → "Request refund", or by raising a support ticket. Support agents can initiate a refund on behalf of an employee in their region.

## 7. Exceptions
Exceptions to the 30-day window require written approval from the Customer Operations lead. Exceptions are not granted for items purchased during clearance sales.
