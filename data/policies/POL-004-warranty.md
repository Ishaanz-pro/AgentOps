# Warranty and Repair Policy (POL-004)

## 1. Coverage
All electronics purchased through the Employee Portal carry a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the delivery date. Furniture carries a 24-month structural warranty.

## 2. What is covered
Manufacturing defects and failures under normal use. Not covered: accidental damage, liquid damage, unauthorised repair, or cosmetic wear.

## 3. Claim process
1. Raise a support ticket describing the fault and including the order ID.
2. Customer Operations issues a return authorisation within 3 business days.
3. The item is repaired or replaced within 15 business days of receipt.

## 4. Relationship to refunds
Items outside the 30-day refund window (POL-001) but inside the warranty period are handled under this policy. A refund is offered only when repair or replacement is not possible.
