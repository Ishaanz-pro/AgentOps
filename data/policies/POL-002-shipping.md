# Shipping, Delivery and Cancellation Policy (POL-002)

## 1. Delivery timelines
- Metro regions (IN-WEST, IN-SOUTH, IN-NORTH metros): 2–4 business days after dispatch.
- Non-metro: 4–7 business days after dispatch.
- Timelines are estimates. Delays caused by carrier disruptions, weather, or regional holidays are not grounds for compensation.

## 2. Carriers
Approved carriers are BlueDart, Delhivery, and Ekart. Tracking IDs are assigned at dispatch and shown on the order record.

## 3. Order statuses
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| processing | Payment confirmed, not yet dispatched |
| shipped | Handed to carrier, tracking ID assigned |
| delivered | Carrier confirmed delivery; delivery date recorded |
| cancelled | Order cancelled by employee or system |

## 4. Cancellation
- `processing` orders can be cancelled instantly from the Employee Portal with a full refund.
- `shipped` orders cannot be cancelled in-transit. The employee may refuse delivery; the parcel is returned and refunded under POL-001 once received at the warehouse.
- `delivered` orders cannot be cancelled; use the refund process in POL-001.

## 5. Lost or damaged parcels
If a parcel is marked `delivered` but not received, the employee must report it within **48 hours** via a support ticket. Logistics opens a carrier investigation; resolution takes up to 10 business days.
